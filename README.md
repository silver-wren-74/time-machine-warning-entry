Entry for the Make.fun competition "Funniest Time Machine Warning Label".

`main.py` writes a single one-line warning label (120 characters max) to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> WARNING: Do not shake hands with yourself. The resulting paradox will be billed to your next of kin.

The joke turns a real time-travel hazard — meeting your duplicate self and
triggering a paradox — into a deadpan official safety notice, saving the
surprise (the paradox is an invoice, sent to your family) for the final words.
